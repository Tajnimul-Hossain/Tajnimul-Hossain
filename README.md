## Tajnimul Hossain

I work as a research assistant in aerial robotics at North South University's NIRO Lab. Using formation control, collision avoidance, and coverage optimization algorithms, I create multi-drone swarm simulations in CoppeliaSim. On NSU-AI-SAT, a 3U CubeSat, I designed the five-mode frequency plan and built the GNU Radio signal chains for the satellite terminal and ground station, and on Project Kingfisher I planned and conducted the CFD analyses characterizing the airframe's aerodynamic performance across design variants. What endures the transition from simulation to actual hardware is what most interests me.

**Tools**

Python, C/C++, GNU Radio, ArduPilot, PX4/Pixhawk, MAVLink, CoppeliaSim, SimScale, MATLAB/Simulink, STM32, Raspberry Pi, RTK GPS.

**Background**

BSc in Electrical and Electronic Engineering, North South University, with a specialization in Robotics and Intelligent Systems.
