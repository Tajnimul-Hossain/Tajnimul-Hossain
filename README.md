## Tajnimul Hossain

I work as a research assistant in aerial robotics at North South University's NIRO Lab. With a four-person team I built a 3-drone swarm on Pixhawk and ArduCopter, after first developing the formation control and collision avoidance in CoppeliaSim. On NSU-AI-SAT, a 3U CubeSat, I co-designed the five-mode frequency plan and built the GNU Radio signal chains for the satellite terminal and ground station. On Project Kingfisher I ran the CFD comparing two ring-wing airframe designs. What survives the move from simulation to real hardware is what interests me most.

**Projects**

- [kingfisher-ringwing-cfd](https://github.com/Tajnimul-Hossain/kingfisher-ringwing-cfd): drag comparison of two ring-wing VTOL airframes in SimScale
- [nsu-ai-sat-comms](https://github.com/Tajnimul-Hossain/nsu-ai-sat-comms): GNU Radio flowgraphs for the five-mode radio of a 3U CubeSat
- [vit-cifar-pytorch](https://github.com/Tajnimul-Hossain/vit-cifar-pytorch): Vision Transformer trained from scratch on CIFAR-10 and CIFAR-100

The swarm code stays private until the work is published.

**Tools**

Python, C/C++, GNU Radio, ArduPilot, PX4/Pixhawk, MAVLink, CoppeliaSim, SimScale, MATLAB/Simulink, STM32, Raspberry Pi, RTK GPS.
